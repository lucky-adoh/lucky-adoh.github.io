Research

My research integrates mechanics, electromechanical energy conversion, sensing and experimental engineering to develop technologies for harvesting ambient energy and monitoring dynamic systems.

Energy Harvesting

Design, modelling and experimental characterisation of piezoelectric and electromagnetic energy harvesting systems, including linear, nonlinear and piecewise-linear architectures. My work examines transducer dynamics, electrical loading, power optimisation, transient response and integration with energy storage and low-power sensing.

Vibration & Structural Dynamics

Analytical and numerical modelling of dynamic systems, vibration measurement, modal and frequency-domain analysis, resonance, damping, nonlinear dynamics and transient excitation. Experimental validation is central to my approach.

Sensors & Structural Health Monitoring

Development and integration of sensing systems for condition monitoring, including self-powered and energy-autonomous approaches, instrumentation, data acquisition and engineering signal processing.

Emerging Research Interests

Energy storage, batteries, energy and functional materials, integrated energy systems, smart infrastructure and sustainable engineering technologies.

Railway & Infrastructure Applications

My applied work includes railway dynamics, train-induced vibration, switches and crossings, track condition assessment, infrastructure monitoring and data-driven maintenance prioritisation.
