Services

I am open to selected research collaborations, technical consulting, and engineering projects in energy harvesting, vibration and structural dynamics, sensing systems, computational modelling, and railway engineering.

My work combines analytical and numerical modelling with experimental methods, providing support from early-stage concept development and simulation through experimental validation and engineering data analysis.

Areas I Can Help With

  • Vibration and structural dynamics including transient response, resonance, damping, modal analysis and dynamic characterisation
  • Energy harvesting systems including piezoelectric and electromagnetic transducers, nonlinear harvesters and electromechanical energy conversion
  • Sensors and self-powered systems including sensor integration, energy-autonomous sensing and structural health monitoring
  • Mathematical and computational modelling of mechanical, structural and coupled electromechanical systems
  • Experimental testing and validation including vibration testing, instrumentation, data acquisition and model validation
  • Signal processing and engineering data analysis including time-domain analysis, FFT, PSD and frequency-response analysis
  • Railway engineering and infrastructure including railway dynamics, train-induced vibration, track condition assessment and infrastructure monitoring
  • Engineering optimisation including parameter studies, sensitivity analysis and design-space exploration
  • Technical and research review including engineering methodology, modelling approaches and experimental design

Collaboration Formats

Research collaborations · Technical consulting · Engineering modelling and simulation · Experimental design and data analysis · Model development and validation · R&D projects · Independent technical review · Academic and industry partnerships.
